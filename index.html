About

I lead solutions technology at The Smarty Train, a London management and transformation consultancy, where I sit on the senior leadership team. I joined in 2021 as Head of Product Design and now look after the tools, platforms and AI work that help our clients — and our own people — change how they work.

Before that I founded Obsrvr, a video coaching platform, and took it through to exit.

My path runs through education and research: digital transformation at the University of Cambridge, product design consulting for Quizlet, a Cambridge PhD spanning education policy and genomics, and an MBA from Keele (with distinction).

These days I spend a lot of time building with AI — agents, internal tools and small apps that remove friction from everyday work.
